#### Research

No detectable edge, and five errors caught and logged. [Dashboard](https://ronaldoexmachina13.github.io/quant-equity-research/)
- **Commodities (MSc thesis, in progress):** is uranium turning from a specialised nuclear-fuel commodity into a financially traded asset? The work measures its integration with gold, oil, copper and silver, tests which market leads price discovery, and asks whether financial participation (UX futures and the Sprott Physical Uranium Trust) changes its sensitivity to the VIX, the US dollar and interest rates.
